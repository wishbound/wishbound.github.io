# Wishbound ✦

**A free wish, pity and spending tracker for LADS players.**
Made by [@pookieleb](https://x.com/pookieleb).

## What it does

- **Wishes:** current balance, free wishes by source, auto daily income (dailies, weeklies, login, Aurum, yearly passes)
- **Pity:** separate counters for limited, rerun, special and standard pools that carry over between banners, with 50/50 and guarantee tracking
- **Pulls:** log pulls per banner, record each 5★ with the pity it dropped at, win/lose, and whether you were going for the card or pulling extra for outfits, poses or ranks
- **Spending:** buy packs and passes in one tap (adds both cost and wishes), monthly budget, planned purchases, spending breakdown, past-spending estimate
- **Banners:** current, upcoming and past banners with a month-by-month wish projection
- **Cards:** your love interests, owned cards and a wishlist for reruns
- Works offline, installs to your home screen, dark mode, CSV export and backup files

Your data is stored only in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Use it

Open the site, go through the short setup, and you're ready. To install it on iPhone/iPad: open it in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
