# Wishbound

**Every wish, accounted for.** A free tracker for Love and Deepspace players: wishes, pity, spending and 5★ Memories in one place.

Made by [@pookieleb](https://x.com/pookieleb).

## What it does

- Wishes and Diamonds, with login income added for you each day
- Pity for limited, rerun, special and Xspace Echo pools, carried over the way the game does it, with 50/50 and guarantee
- Log a session the way you pull: how many wishes, which wish each 5★ dropped on, and when you got the outfit. Extra wishes after getting the Memory are counted separately
- Passes and packs record both what you paid and the wishes you got
- Monthly budget, planned buys, and a look at whether your wishes will last until the next pool
- Every wish pool since launch and every 5★ Memory, with Have and Want for each
- Works offline, installs to your home screen, dark mode, backup files, CSV export

Your data stays in your own browser unless you turn on sync.

## Files

- `js/catalog.js` holds the wish pools and Memories. Add new pools here.
- `js/data.js` holds prices, daily income amounts, yearly pass dates and sync settings.
- `assets/` can hold shared pictures (see `assets/README.md`).
- `supabase/setup.sql` sets up the sync database.
